Privacy Policy

15 doors · iOS App Last updated: October 27, 2026

15 doors ("the App") is a casual game provided by Silrupt. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores on your device, what information is sent to our backend, why it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • available attempts and energy timing;
  • game progress and highest level;
  • unlocked phrase words, themes, and achievements;
  • tool and reward status;
  • gameplay statistics and preferences;
  • pending leaderboard submissions;
  • a cached copy of the global leaderboard.

This information is used to operate the game and preserve your progress between sessions.

2. Anonymous Account and Identifier

The App does not require you to provide a name, email address, password, phone number, or social media account.

When a completed run needs to be submitted to the global leaderboard, the App may create an anonymous account through Supabase. This account is identified by a randomly generated user ID.

The anonymous ID is used to associate your submitted runs, leaderboard position, and optional nickname with the same game account. It is not intended to directly identify you and is not combined by us with your name, email address, advertising identifier, or contacts.

The authentication session is stored in Apple's Keychain on your device.

3. Gameplay and Leaderboard Data

When the App submits a run to the leaderboard, it may send:

  • a randomly generated run ID;
  • run start and end times;
  • run duration;
  • highest level reached and whether the run was completed;
  • lives and tools used;
  • first-try gameplay statistics;
  • App version and anonymous account ID.

This information is used to:

  • calculate leaderboard results and preserve your ranking;
  • prevent duplicate submissions;
  • validate run plausibility and reduce cheating;
  • maintain the game's attempt and energy system;
  • diagnose submission errors.

The App does not send the doors you selected on each level.

4. Public Nicknames and Leaderboard

After reaching the required level, you may choose an optional nickname. Your nickname, leaderboard rank, highest level, and best-level count may be displayed publicly in the App and on related 15 doors services.

Do not use your real name or include personal information in your nickname if you do not want it to be public.

Nicknames are validated and may be renamed, hidden, or removed if they are inappropriate or violate our rules.

5. Limited Product Analytics

The App may record limited product interactions, such as interest in a planned support feature. These events are associated only with the anonymous account ID and are used to understand which App features players are interested in.

We do not use this information for advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling.

6. Motion and Sharing

The App may process accelerometer data on your device to detect a shake gesture used for a hidden in-game reward. Raw motion data is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

If you choose to share a result, the App creates share text and presents Apple's system share sheet. The App does not receive the identity of the recipient or a list of the services you use to share it.

7. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect:

  • your real name, email address, or phone number;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera data;
  • health or fitness information;
  • payment or financial information;
  • browsing history or data for third-party advertising.

There are no advertisements and no in-app purchases in the current version of the App.

8. Service Providers

The App uses Supabase to provide anonymous authentication, run submission, nickname management, leaderboard functionality, and account deletion.

Supabase may process technical request information, such as IP address and request metadata, as necessary to provide, secure, and maintain its service. Supabase processes information under its own privacy and security terms.

The App may contact an Apple-operated website to obtain a trusted time value for the in-game attempt system. Apple may process the request under Apple's Privacy Policy .

We do not allow these services to use App data for advertising on our behalf.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

15 doors does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not perform cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information or use your gameplay data or nickname to build advertising profiles.

✓ No Tracking · No Advertising

10. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it through the App, reset the relevant progress, or remove the App, subject to backups managed through your Apple settings. Leaderboard information remains on our backend while your anonymous leaderboard account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the App:

  1. Open the main screen.
  2. Tap the trophy icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom.
  4. Tap "Delete leaderboard account".
  5. Confirm deletion.

Successful account deletion removes the anonymous account, submitted runs, nickname, and associated leaderboard statistics from our backend. It also resets local game progress on that device.

Deleting the App by itself may not remove the anonymous authentication credential stored in Apple's Keychain. To request complete deletion, use "Delete leaderboard account" before removing the App.

Apple may retain device backups under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

11. App Store Privacy Label

The App Store privacy label for 15 doors may disclose the following categories:

  • Identifiers — anonymous user ID;
  • Usage Data — gameplay and product interaction;
  • User Content — optional public nickname.

These categories are used for App Functionality, are not used for tracking, and are not linked by us to your real-world identity.

12. Data Security

Network communication uses HTTPS/TLS. Authentication credentials are stored using Apple's Keychain. We use access controls and server-side rules intended to prevent unauthorized access and modification.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

15 doors is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so that we can review and delete it.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted at the same public URL and the "Last updated" date will be changed. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of 15 doors after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
