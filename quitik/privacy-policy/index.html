Privacy Policy

quitik · iOS App Last updated: September 2026

quitik ("the App") is a 31-day quit-smoking companion provided by Silrupt. The App works offline. It does not create an account and it does not send your data to a server.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores your journey locally on your iPhone: when you started, hours you have confirmed, stage and achievement progress, and simple on-device counters used by the river scene. If you add the home-screen widget, a small snapshot of that same progress is shared with the widget through an Apple App Group on your device.

We do not receive, collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell, or share this information. It never leaves your device through quitik.

2. Accounts and Identifiers

quitik does not require an account. We do not collect names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, health records, or payment information through the App.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

quitik does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or cross-app tracking. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling. The App does not make network requests as part of its normal operation.

4. Sharing from the App

You may optionally use the iOS share sheet (for example after day 31). That sheet is provided by Apple. What you send, and to whom, is under your control. Silrupt does not receive the shared content.

5. Storage, Deletion, and Backups

Your progress is stored locally using Apple's on-device storage. You can start the journey over from the roadmap screen inside the App. Starting over resets the current run on this device. Achievements from an earlier run may remain marked as earned before.

Deleting the App removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to any device backups managed through your Apple settings. Apple manages device backups under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

6. App Store Privacy Label

quitik's App Store privacy nutrition label is set to:

✓ Data Not Collected

This is the most privacy-protective label available in the App Store, and it accurately reflects how quitik works.

7. Data Security

Because quitik does not operate a server or receive your progress, your information remains under the protections provided by your device and operating system. You are responsible for controlling access to your device and backups.

8. Children's Privacy

quitik is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data from any users, we also collect no data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us at the address below.

9. Health Disclaimer

quitik is a motivational companion. Short notes in the App describe a well-known stop-smoking timeline for general education only. The App is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not nicotine replacement and not a substitute for a doctor or a stop-smoking programme. If you have concerns about your health, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of quitik after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
