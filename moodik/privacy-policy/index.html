Privacy Policy

Moodik · iOS & macOS App Last updated: August 2026

Moodik ("the App") is a private mood and activity tracking application provided by Silrupt.

1. Data You Enter

The App allows you to enter mood values, timestamps, activities, activity names, emojis, and appearance preferences. This information is stored locally on your device.

We do not receive, collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell, or share your mood or activity data.

2. Accounts and Identifiers

Moodik does not require an account. We do not collect names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information through the App.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Moodik does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Demo Data

The App includes synthetic demo mood and activity entries on first launch. Demo data does not describe a real person. You can erase demo data from the Mood screen and add it again from Appearance settings.

5. Storage, Deletion, and Backups

Your entries are stored locally using Apple's on-device storage technology. You can delete supported entries and activities inside the App. You can remove demo data using the "erase demo data" button.

Deleting the App removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to any device backups managed through your Apple settings. Apple manages device backups under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

6. App Store Privacy Label

Moodik's App Store privacy nutrition label is set to:

✓ Data Not Collected

This is the most privacy-protective label available in the App Store, and it accurately reflects how Moodik works.

7. Data Security

Because Moodik does not operate a server or receive your entries, your information remains under the protections provided by your device and operating system. You are responsible for controlling access to your device and backups.

8. Children's Privacy

Moodik is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data from any users, we also collect no data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us at the address below.

9. Health Disclaimer

Moodik is intended for personal reflection and general wellbeing only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have concerns about your mental or physical health, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of Moodik after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
