Privacy Policy

Ragik · macOS App Last updated: March 2026

Ragik is built on a single principle: your thoughts belong to you, and only you. This Privacy Policy explains what data Ragik collects, how it is used, and why the answer to both is essentially: none and it isn't.

Please read this policy carefully. By using Ragik, you agree to the terms described here.

1. What Data Does Ragik Collect?

None.

Ragik does not collect, transmit, process, or store any personal data on external servers. There is no analytics, no crash reporting to third parties, no usage tracking, and no advertising infrastructure of any kind.

2. Where Is My Data Stored?

All data generated by Ragik — including your voice recordings, transcriptions, emotion tags, and weekly summaries — is stored exclusively on your Mac using local on-device storage.

Nothing ever leaves your device. Ragik has no server-side components, no cloud sync, and no remote database.

3. How Does Ragik Use Apple Intelligence?

Ragik uses Apple Intelligence for voice transcription and emotional analysis. Apple Intelligence is a system-level capability built into macOS Tahoe 26.0 and later. All processing happens on your device — no data is sent to Apple's servers or any other external service as part of this functionality.

For more information on how Apple Intelligence handles data, please refer to Apple's own privacy documentation.

4. Do I Need to Create an Account?

No. Ragik does not require any form of registration, sign-in, or account creation. You download it and use it immediately. We have no knowledge of who you are.

5. Does Ragik Share Data with Third Parties?

No. Ragik contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no analytics platforms, and no data-sharing agreements of any kind.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information about you — because we don't have any.

6. App Store Privacy Label

Ragik's App Store privacy nutrition label is set to:

✓ Data Not Collected

This is the most privacy-protective label available in the App Store, and it accurately reflects how Ragik works.

7. Children's Privacy

Ragik is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data from any users, we also collect no data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Continued use of Ragik after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
